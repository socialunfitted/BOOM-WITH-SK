ABOUT BOOM WITH SK

Sivakasi's Heritage, Modernized for Grand Celebrations.

Born in the heart of Sivakasi, Tamil Nadu, BOOM WITH SK (S.K Pyro Park) was founded on a simple principle: every family and event organizer deserves access to genuine, factory-grade fireworks without the inflated 200%–300% local retail markups.

By bridging direct Sivakasi pyrotechnic production with modern logistics, we supply certified green crackers, safe sparklers, multi-shot aerial repeaters, and festive gift boxes directly to clients across India.

01 — SOURCING

Factory-Direct Supply

Manufactured and shipped straight from our Sivakasi production facilities. Zero retail distributors or intermediary markups.

02 — SAFETY

Certified Green Crackers

Formulated under modern CSIR-NEERI guidelines for reduced particulate matter, minimal smoke, and vibrant chromatic brilliance.

03 — LOGISTICS

Burst-Proof Packaging

Rigid triple-wall corrugated cartons with moisture-resistant wrap ensure pristine condition throughout transit.

Transparent wholesale pricing backed by our lowest-price commitment. Found it cheaper? We'll match or beat it.
